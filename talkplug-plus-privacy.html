TalkPlug+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

TalkPlug+ does not collect personal information.
The app does not communicate with external servers. Your settings and practice history are stored only on your device.

1. Information We Collect

TalkPlug+ does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details
  • Location data
  • Contacts, calendars, health data, or data from other apps
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • App usage data or interaction history

No analytics tools or tracking frameworks are included in this app.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Practice settings, practice history, and related app data are stored locally on your device in a storage area dedicated to TalkPlug+. TalkPlug+ does not transmit this data to the developer or to a developer-operated server. If you delete TalkPlug+, this data is also deleted from your device.

Your data may be included in your device's standard iCloud backup if you have backups enabled. TalkPlug+ does not independently sync data to iCloud or any other cloud service.

3. AI Services

TalkPlug+ generates conversation prompts for language practice. It does not provide or operate an AI conversation service.

When you copy a generated prompt and use it with a third-party AI service, any information you provide to that service is governed by that service's own terms of service and privacy policy. TalkPlug+ has no control over, and assumes no responsibility for, how third-party services handle your data.

4. In-App Purchases

TalkPlug+ offers language packs as one-time (non-consumable) in-app purchases through Apple's App Store. All purchase processing is handled by Apple. TalkPlug+ does not receive or store your payment information.

5. Crash Reports

If TalkPlug+ terminates unexpectedly, Apple may make diagnostic information available to the developer when the user has chosen to share analytics and diagnostics with app developers.

The developer may use this information solely to diagnose and fix issues in TalkPlug+.

6. Children

TalkPlug+ does not collect personal information from any user, regardless of age. Accordingly, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page and the date above will be revised. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at uteralab.app@gmail.com.
